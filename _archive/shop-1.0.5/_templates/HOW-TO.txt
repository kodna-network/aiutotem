AIU TOTEM - MAKING SHOP ITEMS
=============================

The shop is just folders on your server. There is no admin login: you add, change or
retire items with DirectAdmin's File Manager, and every Totem sees the change the next
time someone opens the shop.

  public_html/aiu/shop/packs/<item-id>/item.json   what it is, what it costs
  public_html/aiu/shop/packs/<item-id>/item.png    the picture

This _templates folder has one working example for each kind of item, each with a
guide.png that shows exactly where it is drawn in the 192 x 108 scene:

  hat/              worn on the head (pinned to the crown, fits every creature)
  toy/              sits on the ground beside the Totem
  backdrop/         a "view" on the horizon behind everything
  house/            replaces the house outside
  decor/            hangs on the wall inside
  activity-above/   something the Totem does, drawn over its head (juggling)
  activity-side/    ... drawn on the ground to its right (campfire)
  activity-front/   ... drawn over its body (hula hoop)

Folders whose names start with "_" (like this one) are never shown in the shop.


ADD AN ITEM (about 5 minutes)
  1. Copy the template folder closest to what you want, e.g. _templates/hat
  2. Rename the copy to your item id: lowercase letters, numbers and dashes only,
     e.g. pirate-hat. The id must never change after people buy it.
  3. Draw your picture over item.png (keep the name item.png). PNG with a transparent
     background. Aseprite, Piskel (free, in the browser) or Photoshop all work.
     Use the sizes on guide.png. Nothing larger than 192 x 108 is accepted
     (activity strips: up to 768 wide in total).
  4. Edit item.json (File Manager's editor or Notepad):
       {
         "name": "Pirate hat",          shown in the shop (up to 40 letters)
         "category": "hat",             hat, toy, backdrop, house, decor or activity
         "price": 40,                   coins, 0-9999
         "desc": "Arr.",                one short line (optional)
         "file": "item.png"
       }
     Activities also have:
         "frames": 6,                   how many frames are in the strip (1-12)
         "fps": 6,                      animation speed (1-12)
         "anchor": "above",             side, front or above
         "where": "both",               in, out or both (out = not at night or in rain)
         "label": "juggling",           "My Totem is juggling"
         "keys": ["juggle", "circus"]   words that make it a favorite when you mention them
  5. Test it first: open https://kodna.net/aiu/shop/preview.html, pick your PNG and
     item.json, and look at it on a real Totem. Anything red needs fixing.
  6. Upload the folder into public_html/aiu/shop/packs/ (the folder and both files).
     Permissions: folder 755, files 644.
  7. Check: open preview.html > "Check the shop folder". Your item should be listed.
     Folders with a problem are listed in red with the reason (bad JSON, missing
     picture, too big...). You can also open  https://kodna.net/aiu/api.cgi?a=shop


ACTIVITY STRIPS
  Put every frame side by side in one PNG, all the same width, left to right:
     [frame 1][frame 2][frame 3][frame 4]      e.g. 4 frames of 16 x 16 = 64 x 16
  The strip loops forever while the Totem is doing it. Owners also get a
  "Do it now" button in the shop.
    anchor "side":  the frame's bottom-left stands on the ground to the right of the Totem
    anchor "front": the frame is centered on the Totem, its bottom row at the feet
                    (draw only the parts that should be in front, like the near half
                    of a hula hoop)
    anchor "above": the frame is centered over the Totem's head


CHANGE OR RETIRE AN ITEM
  Change the price, name or picture: edit the files. Owners keep what they bought.
  Retire: add  "retired": true  to item.json. It leaves the shop, but anyone who owns it
  keeps it and can still wear it.
  Don't delete a folder people own: their devices keep a copy of the picture, but a
  device that has never downloaded it won't be able to show it.


UPDATING THE APP
  App updates include shop/packs with the 26 starter items. Your own folders are left
  alone as long as you use new ids (extracting the zip only overwrites files with the
  same name).


ART RULES OF THUMB
  - Use the Totem's palette (Sweetie 16): #1a1c2c #5d275d #b13e53 #ef7d57 #ffcd75
    #a7f070 #38b764 #257179 #29366f #3b5dc9 #41a6f6 #73eff7 #f4f4f4 #94b0c2 #566c86
    #333c57, plus earth tones #a0673e #6b3f2a #f2d59a #f5a3b5
  - Hard pixels only: no soft brushes, no anti-aliasing, no half-transparent edges.
  - A 1-pixel dark outline (#1a1c2c) helps small items read on any background.
  - Night darkens everything outside, so check it at 22:00 in preview.html too.
  - Only upload art you are allowed to share: your own, or CC0 packs (e.g. Kenney.nl).
