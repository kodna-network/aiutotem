AIU TOTEM - THE BUTLER'S DICTIONARY
===================================
The butler uses these files to understand what people say. They are not shown to users.

  pocket.txt     offline pocket dictionary (loaded by the app, works with no internet)
  full/*.json    online full dictionary (fetched a piece at a time when the pocket one
                 doesn't know a word)
  places.txt     cities with their location and time zone (city search, weather, and so
                 the butler knows these are places)

Where it comes from (no license involved):
  - Our own word lists, meaning groups, irregular forms and place list, made by KODNA
    for Aiu Totem.
  - Webster's Revised Unabridged Dictionary (1913), which is in the public domain:
    parts of speech and the definitions in full/.
